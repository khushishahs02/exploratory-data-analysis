insights/actions:

what is feature engineering on bmi?
we categorized the bmi column into four standard health weight classes:
1. underweight (bmi < 18.5)
2. normal (18.5 <= bmi < 25)
3. overweight (25 <= bmi < 30)
4. obese (bmi >= 30)

what is one-hot encoding?
one-hot encoding : converting categorical categories into binary columns (0 or 1) for each category.
insights/actions:
1. the bmi category column is one-hot encoded into four new columns: bmi_underweight, bmi_normal, bmi_overweight, bmi_obese.

what is feature scaling?
feature scaling (standardization/z-score normalization) : scaling features to have a mean of 0 and standard deviation of 1. This prevents features with larger magnitudes from dominating the learning process.
insights/actions:
1. standardized the age, bmi, and children columns by subtracting the mean and dividing by the standard deviation.
